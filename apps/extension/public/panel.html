HandsFreeOS Social

Test build. It reads the Facebook page you have open, saves the people, chats and friends you pick to HandsFreeOS, and counts the friend requests, likes, comments and messages you send. It never sends anything on Facebook, and only presses buttons there to get your Facebook file when you ask.

Page got read wrong?

Copy or save the page outline and send it to us. It keeps the page's layout and Facebook's own words, and hides names, messages and posts. Look it over before you send it.
